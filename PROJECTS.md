# 使用 SFL 的项目 | Projects Using SFL

### 关于本列表 | About This List

本页面列出了采用**选择性自由许可证 (SFL) v1.0** 的开源项目。通过使用 SFL，这些项目展示了他们对开源原则的承诺，同时行使排除特定实体的权利。
This page lists open-source projects that have adopted the **Selective Freedom License (SFL) v1.0**. By using SFL, these projects demonstrate their commitment to open-source principles while exercising their right to exclude specific entities.
